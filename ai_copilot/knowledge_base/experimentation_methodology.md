# Experimentation Methodology

The email campaign analysis compares Mens E-Mail and Womens E-Mail treatments against a No E-Mail control group.

Methods:
- Treatment-control comparison
- Two-proportion z-test for conversion rate
- Revenue per user lift
- Bootstrap confidence intervals for revenue lift
- Segment-level treatment effect analysis

A campaign is considered stronger when it improves both conversion and revenue per user with statistical and practical significance.
