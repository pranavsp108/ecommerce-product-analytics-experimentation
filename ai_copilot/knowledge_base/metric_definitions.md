# Metric Definitions

## Revenue
Total purchase revenue from GA4 purchase events.

## Sessions
Distinct GA4 sessions identified by user and session ID.

## Active Users
Distinct users with observed activity in the analysis window.

## Purchase Conversion Rate
Purchasing sessions divided by total sessions.

Formula:
purchasing_sessions / sessions

## Average Order Value
Total revenue divided by number of purchase events or transactions.

## Cart Abandonment Rate
Sessions with add-to-cart activity that did not complete purchase.

## Revenue per User
Total revenue divided by users.

## Revenue per Session
Total revenue divided by sessions.

## Week 1 Retention
Users active in week 1 after acquisition divided by users in week 0.
