## 1. Executive Summary

The e-commerce platform generated $362.17K in revenue across 360,129 sessions and 270,154 active users, with an overall purchase conversion rate of 1.35%.

The largest product opportunity is early-funnel discovery: only 21.4% of sessions reached a product-view event. Customer value is highly concentrated, with high-value customers representing a small user group but contributing a large share of revenue.

## 2. Key KPI Highlights

- Revenue: $362.17K
- Sessions: 360,129
- Active users: 270,154
- Purchase conversion rate: 1.35%
- Average order value: $68.88
- Cart abandonment rate: 21.08%

## 3. Risks and Opportunities

- Product discovery is the largest funnel gap.
- Checkout-to-purchase conversion still shows friction.
- One-time buyers and high-value customers are the strongest monetization opportunities.
- Cart and checkout abandoners are strong candidates for recovery campaigns.

## 4. Modeling and Experimentation Insights

The Random Forest purchase propensity model achieved 0.847 ROC-AUC and 0.040 PR-AUC, representing roughly 15.8x lift over the baseline future-purchase rate.

The email experiment showed positive conversion and revenue lift. Mens E-Mail was the strongest variant, generating +0.68 percentage-point conversion lift and +$0.77 revenue per user vs control.

## 5. Recommended Actions

- Improve product discovery and product-page relevance.
- Prioritize high-value customers for retention.
- Target one-time buyers with second-purchase campaigns.
- Use propensity scores to rank users for retargeting.
- Roll out Mens E-Mail while continuing targeted testing.
